Drawing

How to draw

  • Your right index fingertip is the pen.
  • It draws while you hold Shift or bring the left hand's five fingertips together. In Scaffold mode you stretch a straight line; in Shape mode you draw a curve.
  • Tab or the right hand's five fingertips together switches Scaffold ↔ Shape.
  • Make fists with both hands and move them to move, turn and zoom the drawing.
  • A very short scaffold line across a line adds a keypoint (tick mark) there.
  • Shape strokes snap to scaffold keypoints (dots: ends, middles, ticks) and follow scaffold directions.
  • In VR, turn your open left palm toward you for a menu; poke its buttons.
  • Mouse: left-drag (or Shift) draws on a plane through the view's center; right-drag orbits; scroll moves the view in and out.
