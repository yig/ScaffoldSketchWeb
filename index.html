How to draw

  • Pinch (thumb + index) with your drawing hand to draw. In Scaffold mode you stretch a straight line; in Shape mode you draw a curve.
  • A very short scaffold line across a line adds a keypoint (tick mark) there.
  • Shape strokes snap to scaffold keypoints (dots: ends, middles, ticks) and follow scaffold directions.
  • Quick pinch with the other hand switches Scaffold ↔ Shape.
  • Pinch and move the other hand to move the drawing; pinch with both hands to zoom and turn it.
  • In VR, turn your other palm toward you for a menu; poke its buttons.
  • Mouse: left-drag draws on a plane through the view's center; right-drag orbits; scroll moves the view in and out.
